## ODR-Application-1 Application

Orders for physical goods are accepted for delivery to serviceable Singapore addresses, subject to the delivery option, charges and conditions confirmed at checkout. This Policy governs delivery services, shipping fees, changes to orders, tracking, partial deliveries and delivery problems. A request to change or cancel an order does not constitute confirmation that the change or cancellation has taken effect. All dates use Singapore time. Calendar days include weekends and public holidays. Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and Singapore public holidays. Delivery periods and service targets are estimates unless an express commitment has been confirmed for the order.

## SHP-ShippingOptions-2.1 Available delivery services

The following doorstep delivery services may be available at checkout: (i) standard delivery, normally taking two (2) to five (5) business days after carrier pickup; and (ii) express delivery, normally taking one (1) to two (2) business days after carrier pickup. Availability depends on the delivery address and the goods purchased. The service applicable to an existing order is the service recorded for that order; the courier's name alone does not establish the service purchased. International delivery, lockers, collection points, installation and scheduled delivery appointments are not included in the standard offering. A request concerning a service not confirmed for the order requires review rather than an assumed entitlement.

## SHP-ShippingOptions-2.2 Delivery periods and appointments

The estimated periods in section 2.1 begin when the carrier accepts the parcel, not when the order is placed, paid or assigned a label. Where the shipment has an updated estimated delivery date, that shipment-specific date takes precedence over a general service estimate. A specific arrival time cannot be reserved through the standard delivery service. The customer should arrange for an authorized recipient where necessary. An estimate does not guarantee delivery on a particular date, and an unsupported revised date must not be presented as confirmed.

Related clauses: SHP-ShippingOptions-2.1.

## SHP-ShippingOptions-2.3 Changes to the shipping option

A customer may request a service change before fulfillment begins under section 4.1. A change is subject to: (a) availability for the replacement service; (b) serviceability of the delivery address; (c) acceptance of any revised shipping charge; and (d) confirmation that the amendment has been recorded. Once fulfillment begins, a service upgrade or expedited handling is not guaranteed and requires human review. A request alone does not amend the purchased service.

Related clauses: ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.1.

## SHP-ShippingFees-3.1 Applicable charge

The shipping fee for a prospective purchase is the charge confirmed at checkout. For an existing purchase, the applicable fee is the amount recorded on that order, including any recorded discount and tax components. Historical charges are not recalculated using current catalog prices. A free-shipping threshold or loyalty benefit applies only where it formed part of the purchase terms. A customer disputing a shipping charge may submit the order reference and relevant payment information for review.

## SHP-ShippingFees-3.2 Separate parcels and additional charges

An order may be divided into several parcels without creating an additional shipping charge. The original fee must not be multiplied by the number of shipments or tracking numbers. A suspected duplicate charge is subject to payment review. Two pending authorizations do not necessarily represent two settled charges, and the same amount will not be refunded twice.

## SHP-ShippingFees-3.3 Shipping fees on cancellation or return

For an accepted full cancellation before fulfillment, the refund calculation includes the original recorded shipping and tax components, subject to the captured balance and earlier refunds. For an accepted whole-order return due to store fault, the recorded outbound shipping fee is refundable once. For change-of-mind returns, the original outbound fee is normally retained. A partial return does not automatically make the whole outbound fee refundable. Delay alone does not establish an automatic entitlement to compensation or a shipping-fee refund. Disputed fees and missing allocations require review.

## ODR-OrderStatusTracking-5.1 Order and parcel status

An order may be processing, shipped, partially fulfilled, delivered or cancelled. These statuses describe different stages and must be considered together with the associated shipment records. In particular: (a) processing does not necessarily mean warehouse work has not begun; (b) a shipping label does not establish carrier pickup; (c) delivery of one parcel does not establish delivery of all items; and (d) several tracking numbers do not necessarily indicate duplicate orders. Order references, shipment references and carrier tracking numbers identify different records. Customer-specific order, address and payment information is available only after the customer's authority to access the order has been established; possession of an order reference alone is insufficient.

## ODR-OrderStatusTracking-5.2 Missing or invalid tracking information

A missing scan or invalid tracking number may reflect an unupdated record, an incorrect reference or a carrier-system issue. It does not, by itself, establish cancellation or loss. The latest confirmed shipping event and any discrepancy should be used when enquiring about progress. An update, carrier contact or expedited handling must not be presented as completed unless it has been confirmed.

## ODR-OrderStatusTracking-5.3 Estimated delivery date

The latest estimated date recorded for the affected shipment is the applicable estimate. If no shipment-specific date exists, a general period under section 2.1 may be given only where the service and pickup date are established. An estimated date is not proof that delivery occurred. Missing dates and conflicting status information require clarification or review, not substitution of an assumed arrival date.

Related clauses: SHP-ShippingOptions-2.1.

## SHP-SeparateShipPartialDel-6.1 Orders containing several parcels

Goods from one order may be dispatched separately, with different shipment references, tracking numbers and arrival dates. The order remains a single purchase unless the transaction records show otherwise. Each parcel's progress is assessed separately. A parcel delivered before the others does not establish that the remaining quantities are missing. Splitting the order does not create a further delivery charge under section 3.2.

Related clauses: SHP-ShippingFees-3.2.

## SHP-SeparateShipPartialDel-6.2 Missing items and outstanding parcels

Where only part of an order has arrived, the goods expected in each parcel must be considered. If an absent item is allocated to an outstanding parcel, the delivery provisions in section 7 apply to that parcel. If the item should have been inside a delivered parcel, the customer may raise an incomplete-delivery claim for the affected quantities. Where the allocation or contents are disputed or cannot be established, the matter requires clarification or human review.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.1, SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.2, SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.3, SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.4, SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.5.

## SHP-SeparateShipPartialDel-6.3 Return dates and affected quantities

For separate deliveries, each item's return period begins when the parcel containing that item is delivered. The earliest delivery in the order does not determine the deadline for every item. A return or refund concerns only the accepted affected quantities. Retained goods, outstanding parcels and unrelated items do not become refundable solely because another item is returned. Any refund is based on the amount paid for the affected quantities, including recorded discount and tax allocations, less amounts already refunded or pending refund.

## SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.1 Delayed deliveries

A parcel is delayed if it has not arrived after the latest recorded estimated delivery date. A missing or late scan alone is not confirmation of loss. Where the parcel remains undelivered more than three (3) calendar days after that estimate, the case will be referred for human investigation. A customer may request human assistance sooner. If no reliable estimate exists, the matter requires review rather than an invented waiting-period end date. The ordinary waiting period does not apply to confirmed loss or transit damage under section 7.2, or to a delivered-but-not-received claim under section 7.4.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.2, SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.4.

## SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.2 Confirmed loss or transit damage

Notwithstanding the ordinary waiting period in section 7.1, a carrier declaration of loss or damage in transit requires immediate human review. The affected parcels and quantities, and any existing claim, will be considered. A customer allegation alone does not constitute a carrier declaration. Confirmed non-delivery may support a refund without physical return following human review. Opening an investigation does not itself approve a refund or replacement.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.1.

## SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.3 Failed delivery and return to sender

An unsuccessful attempt is subject to the recorded reason and the carrier's confirmed redelivery or collection arrangement. Another attempt, appointment or collection deadline is not guaranteed unless confirmed. Where the parcel is returning to sender, the outcome and any refund are subject to review of the reason and the parcel's disposition. An unavailable recipient or an incorrect address does not automatically establish store fault. Shipping-fee treatment depends on the accepted resolution.

## SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.4 Delivered but not received

Where tracking shows delivery but the customer denies receipt, the claim will be referred for human investigation immediately. The ordinary waiting period in section 7.1 does not apply. Relevant circumstances may include: (i) whether a household member or authorized recipient accepted the parcel; (ii) the recorded delivery location and time; (iii) any delivery photograph, signature or safe-place confirmation; and (iv) whether the confirmation concerns the correct parcel and address. A delivery scan or the existence of a photograph alone is not conclusive proof of correct receipt. The customer is not required to carry out an unsafe search. Unverified or conflicting evidence remains subject to human review.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.1.

## SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.5 Outcome of delivery investigation

A confirmed finding of non-delivery may support a refund for the affected goods. The amount is limited to the amount paid for those goods and any approved shipping and tax components, less amounts already refunded or pending refund. Where delivery is confirmed or remains disputed, the customer may seek review of the decision and relevant evidence. An investigation, accepted resolution and completed payment are separate events. A refund, reshipment, carrier contact or address amendment must not be treated as completed solely because it was requested or accepted for review.
