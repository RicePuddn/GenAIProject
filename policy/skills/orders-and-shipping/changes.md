## ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.1 Changes before fulfillment

A cancellation, address correction or delivery-service change may be accepted while the order is processing and fulfillment has not begun. For this purpose, the ordinary change period ends when any of the following occurs: (i) a shipping label is generated; (ii) warehouse packing begins; (iii) any quantity is fulfilled; or (iv) the parcel is collected by the carrier. A processing status alone does not establish that the change period is still open. A label may exist before a pickup scan appears. Where the relevant status is unavailable or contradictory, the request requires human review. An ordinary cancellation may be recorded automatically where the customer's authority has been verified, the order is confirmed to be processing and every fulfillment condition above is confirmed not to have occurred. A request with missing or conflicting facts must not be treated as an approved cancellation. Any resulting refund must separately satisfy section 4.2; cancellation approval alone does not approve payment.

Related clauses: ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.2.

## ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.2 Cancellation and refund

An eligible cancellation takes effect only when confirmation is issued and the order record is updated. The refundable amount following a full cancellation is the captured balance, including recorded original shipping and tax, less any amount already refunded or pending refund. An order that was not paid does not create a refund entitlement. A pending authorization may require release rather than repayment of settled funds. A cancellation confirmation does not establish that the payment has already been returned; refunds are returned to the original payment method. Initiation is targeted within two (2) business days after final approval and required checks, with an estimated five (5) to ten (10) business days for supported card and PayNow payments to appear after processing. The following approval requirements apply only where the cancellation and the payment checks above have been completed: (a) an eligible refund of SGD 30.00 or less may be processed automatically; (b) an eligible refund above SGD 30.00 and up to SGD 100.00 requires automated review of the verified facts, cancellation and refundable amount before payment is approved; and (c) a refund above SGD 100.00 requires human review before payment is approved. Any other requirement for human review takes precedence regardless of amount. Missing or conflicting facts must not be treated as approval. These limits do not increase the captured balance available for refund or permit a duplicate payment.

## ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.3 Requests after fulfillment begins

Cancellation or amendment after fulfillment begins is subject to human review and cannot be guaranteed. Once goods have been delivered, a request to send them back is a return request rather than a pre-dispatch cancellation. An already cancelled order must not be cancelled again. Any financial query concerning it remains subject to the original payment and refund history.

## ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.4 Delivery address corrections

Before fulfillment, a customer may request correction of the delivery address by providing the complete replacement Singapore address. The correction is subject to verification of the customer's authority, serviceability and confirmation of the amendment. After fulfillment begins, redirection is not guaranteed, including where a label exists but pickup has not occurred. A request will be referred for human review. The original address remains the recorded destination until an amendment is confirmed. An incorrect-address report does not automatically establish loss or store fault. Failed delivery follows section 7.3; a delivered-but-not-received claim follows section 7.4.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.3, SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.4.

## ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.5 Additional products or quantities

Products cannot be added to a paid order and paid quantities cannot be increased through an ordinary amendment. Additional goods require a new order. If the customer wishes to cancel and reorder instead, the original order must independently satisfy section 4.1. Cancellation is not guaranteed merely because a new order is intended.

Related clauses: ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.1.
