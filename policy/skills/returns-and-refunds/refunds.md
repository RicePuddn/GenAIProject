## REF-Refunds-6.1 Conditions for payment

A refund is issued after the request has been accepted and, where a physical return is required, the returned goods have been received and the applicable inspection completed. The inspection considers the identity, quantities, completeness and conditions relevant to the original return reason, including any exception in section 2.5. The store targets inspection within three (3) business days after warehouse receipt. Return tracking marked delivered does not itself establish that inspection is complete. A delay or a disputed finding may be raised for review under section 7. A return recorded as requested or received may still be progressing through the ordinary return process. That status alone does not require human review or establish that a refund is due. Routine progress enquiries may be answered automatically using the confirmed records. A disputed finding, unavailable required evidence or a request for human assistance must be referred for human review. Physical return may be waived following human review where non-delivery is confirmed or return is unsafe or impractical. The waiver applies only to the goods and resolution specified. It does not remove the requirement to establish the payment and refundable amount.

## REF-Refunds-6.2 Refund amount

The amount is based on the approved affected quantities and the amount paid for those goods, including the applicable recorded discount and tax allocations. It is not calculated using a later catalog price. Shipping components for returns are governed by section 5 of this Policy. For an accepted full cancellation before fulfillment, the recorded original shipping and tax are included, subject to the captured balance and earlier refunds. A partial return does not entitle the customer to reimbursement of retained or unaffected goods. Where the value of an accessory, a discount allocation or another component cannot be established, the amount requires review rather than an assumed allocation.

Related clauses: RET-ShippingFees-5.1, RET-ShippingFees-5.2.

## REF-Refunds-6.3 Payment destination and previous refunds

Refunds are made in SGD to the original payment method. They cannot be redirected to a different account or payment instrument supplied during a support conversation. The total of completed and pending refunds must not exceed the amount captured for the order. Amounts already refunded or reserved for an existing refund will be deducted from the remaining balance. Failed payments and uncaptured authorizations cannot be refunded as settled payments. Approval of a return does not authorize a duplicate refund.

## REF-Refunds-6.4 Refund timeframe

The store targets refund initiation within two (2) business days after final acceptance and completion of the required receipt and inspection, or an approved waiver. Return authorization alone does not start this period. After processing, supported card and PayNow refunds normally appear within five (5) to ten (10) business days. These are estimated periods rather than guaranteed posting dates. Requested, accepted and pending refunds may not yet have been processed.

## REF-Refunds-6.5 Delayed refunds and other resolutions

Where the relevant period has passed or the recorded status conflicts with the payment received, the customer may request further review. An additional refund will not be issued solely because an earlier refund is not yet visible. Exchange, replacement, repair, store credit and goodwill compensation require separate agreement. A request for one of these alternatives does not guarantee stock, dispatch or payment.

## REF-Refunds-6.6 Refund approval requirements

The approval requirements below apply only after the acceptance, inspection and payment conditions in sections 6.1, 6.2 and 6.3 have been satisfied: (a) an eligible refund of SGD 30.00 or less may be processed automatically; (b) an eligible refund above SGD 30.00 and up to SGD 100.00 requires automated review before payment is approved; and (c) a refund above SGD 100.00 requires human review before payment is approved. Automated review must establish that the proposed action is supported by verified case facts, the applicable policy clauses and the approved refundable amount. A proposal that cannot be substantiated must be corrected or referred for human review; the amount alone does not establish eligibility. A requirement for human review elsewhere in this Policy takes precedence regardless of amount. In particular, disputed findings, unavailable or unsafe evidence and unapproved exceptions must not be resolved by automatic payment. An automatically issued return authorization does not establish that these payment requirements have been satisfied. The approval limits do not increase the captured balance available for refund, permit duplicate payment or allow a different payment destination.
