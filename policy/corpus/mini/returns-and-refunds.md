# Returns and refunds

## RET-1 Return window
Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery when the item is unused and in its original packaging. Verify the order and delivery date with read tools before advising eligibility. If required facts are missing, ask the customer or hand off; do not infer them.

## RET-1-EX-1 Final-sale items
Exception to: RET-1
Marked final-sale items are not returnable.

## REF-1 Refund method and approval
Refunds are issued to the original payment method after an eligible return is received. A refund above the mock approval limit requires human review. Never state a refund was issued unless the ticket-state tool confirms the mock write.
