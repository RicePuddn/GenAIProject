## 4. Return procedure

### RET-ReturnProcedure-4.1 Return authorization

Goods must not be dispatched until a return authorization and return destination have been provided. The authorization identifies the goods, quantities and approved shipping arrangement. Permission to send goods back is not confirmation that inspection has passed or a refund has been processed. An ordinary return authorization may be issued automatically where the affected goods and quantities are identified, the applicable eligibility and condition requirements in section 2 have been verified, the evidence requirements in section 3.1 are satisfied and no condition requires human review. Missing facts must be clarified; unavailable, inaccessible or unsafe evidence follows section 3.2. Authorization concerns permission to return the specified goods and does not authorize payment.

### RET-ReturnProcedure-4.2 Dispatch deadline

Authorized goods must be handed to the carrier within seven (7) calendar days after approval of the return, unless an extension under section 4.5 applies. The approval date is day zero and the seventh day is included. Carrier acceptance or a drop-off receipt establishes dispatch. Creation of a label alone does not establish that the goods were sent. The dispatch deadline concerns handover, not arrival at the warehouse; delivery to the warehouse after that deadline does not necessarily mean dispatch was late.

Related clauses: RET-ReturnProcedure-4.5.

### RET-ReturnProcedure-4.3 Shipping arrangements and amendments

The return may use an authorized pickup, prepaid label or customer-arranged shipment, as specified for the case. A customer-arranged shipment is reimbursable only under the conditions in section 5.1. Changes to the method, collection date or return details may be requested before carrier handover and take effect only when confirmed. Once the goods have been handed over, changes are subject to the carrier's handling and are not guaranteed. A pickup or label must not be treated as arranged until confirmation has been issued.

Related clauses: RET-ShippingFees-5.1.

### RET-ReturnProcedure-4.4 Condition and packaging

Goods must be packed securely to prevent damage or loss during return. The return must include the authorized quantities and the accessories, components and gifts required by the approved resolution. The customer should retain the return reference and proof of handover. Original packaging remains required for a change-of-mind return. Where section 2.5 applies and original packaging is damaged or unavailable, suitable protective packaging may be used instead. A claim concerning an original fault must not be rejected solely because the original packaging is absent.

Related clauses: RET-ReturnEligibility-2.5.

### RET-ReturnProcedure-4.5 Extensions and failed collection

A recorded human-approved extension replaces the ordinary dispatch deadline only for the named return and the new date specified. A failed store-arranged pickup may support a request for an extension but does not create one automatically. Late or unverified dispatch requires human review. A customer must not be told that the return was automatically cancelled without a recorded cancellation or decision.

### RET-ReturnProcedure-4.6 Unsafe returns

Leaking goods, unsafe batteries and other goods presenting a handling risk require human review and specific return instructions. Customers should not dispatch such goods using an ordinary return arrangement or dispose of them unless expressly instructed to do so.
