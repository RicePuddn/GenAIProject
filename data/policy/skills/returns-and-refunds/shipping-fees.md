## 5. Outbound and return-shipping fees

### RET-ShippingFees-5.1 Cost of return shipment

For a verified store-fault return, the store bears the approved return-shipping cost using the authorized arrangement. Where the customer arranges shipment, reimbursement requires: (i) prior approval of the method and cost; and (ii) evidence of the amount actually paid. For a change-of-mind return, return shipping is normally the customer's responsibility. The applicable charge or responsibility must be explained before handover. Unconfirmed fault or disputed charges require further review before reimbursement is agreed.

### RET-ShippingFees-5.2 Original outbound shipping

The original outbound shipping fee is normally not refunded for a change-of-mind return. Where an approved store-fault resolution affects the whole order, the recorded outbound fee is included once. For a partial return, any outbound-fee refund depends on a recorded allocation or an expressly approved amount. The whole fee is not automatically refundable for each returned item. For an accepted full cancellation before fulfillment, the refund includes the recorded original shipping and tax, less amounts already refunded or pending refund.
