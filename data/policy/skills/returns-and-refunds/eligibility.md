## RET-Application-1 Application for returns and refunds

Subject to the conditions in this Policy, customers may apply for the return of purchased goods and/or a refund. A request may concern goods received by the customer or goods that have not been received. Submission of a request does not, by itself, constitute acceptance of the return or approval of a refund. This Policy applies to physical goods purchased in Singapore dollars for delivery within Singapore. The same standard conditions apply to all customer loyalty tiers. All dates are assessed in Singapore time. Calendar days include weekends and public holidays. Where a period is stated in business days, Saturdays, Sundays and Singapore public holidays are excluded. A specific exception changes only the condition expressly identified; the remaining conditions continue to apply.

## 2. Application for the return of goods

### RET-ReturnEligibility-2.1 Goods received by the customer

A customer may submit a return request within thirty (30) calendar days after delivery of the affected goods. The delivery date is day zero, and a request submitted on the thirtieth day is within the ordinary request period. The period runs from actual delivery, not the order date, payment date or dispatch date. Where an order is delivered in separate parcels, each affected item's period runs from delivery of the parcel containing that item. Delivery of another parcel from the same order does not shorten the period. If an item's delivery date or parcel allocation cannot be established, the request will require clarification or human review. The date the request was first recorded determines whether it was submitted on time. A timely request does not become late because inspection or review takes place later. However, timely submission does not waive the conditions relating to evidence, item condition or return shipment. For example, goods delivered on 5 October have an ordinary request deadline of 4 November, inclusive. Requests outside the ordinary return period are addressed in section 2.6.

### RET-ReturnEligibility-2.2 Goods not received by the customer

A customer may raise a non-receipt claim where: (i) the estimated delivery date has passed and the parcel has not arrived; (ii) tracking shows delivery but the customer has not received the parcel; or (iii) the carrier has confirmed that the parcel was lost or damaged in transit. Non-receipt claims require investigation of the shipment status, delivery records and any carrier findings. A non-receipt claim does not require the customer to return goods that they do not possess. A refund remains subject to the investigation outcome and the conditions in section 6 of this Policy.

Related clauses: REF-Refunds-6.1, REF-Refunds-6.2, REF-Refunds-6.3, REF-Refunds-6.4, REF-Refunds-6.5, REF-Refunds-6.6.

### RET-ReturnEligibility-2.3 Reasons for a return request

A return and/or refund may be requested in the following circumstances: (a) the parcel is empty or the quantity received is less than the quantity purchased; (b) supplied accessories or components are missing; (c) the goods differ from the purchased item or variant, such as a different model, size or colour; (d) the goods are defective or do not function as intended; (e) the goods sustained physical damage before or during delivery; or (f) the customer wishes to return goods in their original, unused condition due to a change of mind. The reason selected must correspond to the issue reported. A concern that goods may be counterfeit is subject to human review under section 7.3 rather than an automatic refund or rejection. Before goods are treated as missing, any outstanding parcels from the same order must be taken into account.

Related clauses: RET-ReturnParcelDisputes-7.3.

### RET-ReturnEligibility-2.4 Change-of-mind returns

A change-of-mind return is available within the period in section 2.1 only where the goods: (i) are unused and in their original condition; (ii) remain in their original packaging; (iii) retain original tags or labels, where supplied; (iv) include all accessories, components and gifts supplied with the purchase; and (v) were not designated final sale at the time of purchase. The affected goods and quantities must be identified in the request. Goods returned as part of an approved bundle must include the components specified in that return authorization. A change in preference is not treated as a defect where the goods match the purchased specification. The conditions for damaged, faulty or incorrect goods, including exceptions to final-sale and original-packaging requirements, are set out in section 2.5.

Related clauses: RET-ReturnEligibility-2.1, RET-ReturnEligibility-2.5.

### RET-ReturnEligibility-2.5 Final-sale goods and the store-fault exception

Goods marked final sale at the time of purchase are excluded from change-of-mind returns. A product's general category or a label added after purchase does not establish that it was sold as final sale. Notwithstanding that exclusion, a request concerning verified arrival damage, a defect or an incorrect item may be accepted within the period in section 2.1. In those circumstances, the unused and original-packaging conditions may be waived where opening, testing or loss of packaging was reasonably connected with discovering or reporting the fault. Secure return packaging is still required where the goods are to be returned. This exception does not cover damage caused by later misuse, extend the thirty-day request period or establish an entitlement to a refund without assessment. Evidence and any required inspection remain applicable. Missing original packaging alone is not a sufficient reason to reject a qualifying damaged-item claim.

Related clauses: RET-ReturnEligibility-2.1.

### RET-ReturnEligibility-2.6 Requests outside the ordinary conditions

A request submitted after thirty days will be referred for human review of the stated circumstances. It is not automatically accepted or rejected. Any approved exception must identify the goods and the condition or deadline being varied. Contact with customer service or an unanswered earlier message does not, by itself, establish an approved extension. Digital goods, services and standalone promotional gifts are outside the standard physical-return process. A claim concerning such a purchase requires human review; absence of a physical-return process does not automatically determine whether a separate refund claim is valid.
