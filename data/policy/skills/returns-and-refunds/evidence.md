## 3. Supporting evidence

### RET-SupportingEvidence-3.1 Evidence relevant to the request

Customers should provide the order reference, affected goods and quantities, a description of the issue and relevant supporting evidence. Depending on the reason, this may include: (a) photographs of visible damage, the goods and their packaging; (b) photographs of the goods received and their identifying labels where an incorrect item is alleged; (c) photographs or packing information showing the contents of an incomplete parcel; or (d) a description and, where safe and practical, a demonstration of a malfunction. Customers must not operate unsafe goods to demonstrate a defect. Non-receipt is assessed using shipment and investigation evidence rather than a photograph of goods that are absent. Passwords, full payment-card details and unrelated personal information are not required. Where evidence cannot be provided, accessed or safely obtained, the exception in section 3.2 applies.

Related clauses: RET-SupportingEvidence-3.2.

### RET-SupportingEvidence-3.2 Unavailable, inaccessible or unsafe evidence

If evidence is unavailable, inaccessible or unsafe to produce, the limitation must be recorded and the request referred for human review instead of automatic rejection. This is an exception to the ordinary evidence requirements in section 3.1. An uploaded attachment is not automatically accepted as verified evidence. A human reviewer will determine whether the available material is sufficient. Referral for review does not itself approve the return or refund.

Related clauses: RET-SupportingEvidence-3.1.

### RET-SupportingEvidence-3.3 Clarification and existing requests

Further relevant information may be requested where the issue, delivery date, quantities or condition remains unclear. Customer statements, inspection findings and confirmed transaction details will be considered separately; a disputed statement is not treated as an established fact merely because it appears in a request. Additional evidence concerning an existing case should be attached to that case. Repeated submissions for the same goods do not create separate refund entitlements. Any earlier approval remains limited to the goods, quantities and resolution it expressly covered.
