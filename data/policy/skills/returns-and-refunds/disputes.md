## 7. Return-parcel disputes

### RET-ReturnParcelDisputes-7.1 Grounds for a dispute

A dispute may be raised concerning: (a) a return parcel reported as not received; (b) missing goods, quantities, accessories or bundle components; (c) a different item in the returned parcel; (d) damage, a fault or a condition inconsistent with the accepted return reason; (e) the authenticity of purchased or returned goods; or (f) whether a change-of-mind return meets the applicable condition requirements. A dispute initiates a review. It does not establish that either party's account is correct or create a second refund entitlement.

### RET-ReturnParcelDisputes-7.2 Incomplete, damaged, faulty or wrong goods

The original return reason, purchase details, supporting evidence and inspection findings will be considered together. Review must distinguish the original reported fault from an allegation of additional damage, missing contents or a different item during the return. The customer is not automatically responsible for damage merely because the goods were returned damaged. A qualifying original fault remains subject to section 2.5. Conflicting accounts or inspection findings will be referred for human review rather than automatic refund or rejection.

Related clauses: RET-ReturnEligibility-2.5.

### RET-ReturnParcelDisputes-7.3 Counterfeit products

A claim concerning authenticity will be referred for human review. Relevant product, packaging and purchase evidence may be requested. A brand name, low price, image or allegation alone does not establish that goods are genuine or counterfeit. Any accepted resolution will identify the affected goods, whether physical return is required and the applicable refund. No special compensation or determination of authenticity is implied merely by raising the claim.

### RET-ReturnParcelDisputes-7.4 Goods returned due to change of mind

A dispute may arise where inspection reports use, missing original packaging or tags, missing accessories, an incomplete bundle or a different item. The applicable conditions are those in section 2.4 and the agreed return authorization. If the customer contests the finding, the request will be referred for human review. Where the original reason concerns arrival damage, a defect or an incorrect item, section 2.5 must be considered rather than applying change-of-mind conditions without that exception.

Related clauses: RET-ReturnEligibility-2.4, RET-ReturnEligibility-2.5.

### RET-ReturnParcelDisputes-7.5 Review and communication

The store may request further relevant evidence and will communicate the reviewed outcome and its reason. An unresolved allegation is not proof of misuse, fraud or ineligibility. Unavailable or unsafe evidence is handled under section 3.2. A customer may request human review of a disputed decision. Existing case details and additional evidence should remain associated with the same request. Contact with support is not, by itself, confirmation that an investigation, amendment or payment has been completed.

Related clauses: RET-SupportingEvidence-3.2.
