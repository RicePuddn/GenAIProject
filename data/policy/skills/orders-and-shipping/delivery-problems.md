## 7. Delivery problems

### SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.1 Delayed deliveries

A parcel is delayed if it has not arrived after the latest recorded estimated delivery date. A missing or late scan alone is not confirmation of loss. Where the parcel remains undelivered more than three (3) calendar days after that estimate, the case will be referred for human investigation. A customer may request human assistance sooner. If no reliable estimate exists, the matter requires review rather than an invented waiting-period end date. The ordinary waiting period does not apply to confirmed loss or transit damage under section 7.2, or to a delivered-but-not-received claim under section 7.4.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.2, SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.4.

### SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.2 Confirmed loss or transit damage

Notwithstanding the ordinary waiting period in section 7.1, a carrier declaration of loss or damage in transit requires immediate human review. The affected parcels and quantities, and any existing claim, will be considered. A customer allegation alone does not constitute a carrier declaration. Confirmed non-delivery may support a refund without physical return following human review. Opening an investigation does not itself approve a refund or replacement.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.1.

### SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.3 Failed delivery and return to sender

An unsuccessful attempt is subject to the recorded reason and the carrier's confirmed redelivery or collection arrangement. Another attempt, appointment or collection deadline is not guaranteed unless confirmed. Where the parcel is returning to sender, the outcome and any refund are subject to review of the reason and the parcel's disposition. An unavailable recipient or an incorrect address does not automatically establish store fault. Shipping-fee treatment depends on the accepted resolution.

### SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.4 Delivered but not received

Where tracking shows delivery but the customer denies receipt, the claim will be referred for human investigation immediately. The ordinary waiting period in section 7.1 does not apply. Relevant circumstances may include: (i) whether a household member or authorized recipient accepted the parcel; (ii) the recorded delivery location and time; (iii) any delivery photograph, signature or safe-place confirmation; and (iv) whether the confirmation concerns the correct parcel and address. A delivery scan or the existence of a photograph alone is not conclusive proof of correct receipt. The customer is not required to carry out an unsafe search. Unverified or conflicting evidence remains subject to human review.

Related clauses: SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.1.

### SHP-DeliveryProblems-7.5 Outcome of delivery investigation

A confirmed finding of non-delivery may support a refund for the affected goods. The amount is limited to the amount paid for those goods and any approved shipping and tax components, less amounts already refunded or pending refund. Where delivery is confirmed or remains disputed, the customer may seek review of the decision and relevant evidence. An investigation, accepted resolution and completed payment are separate events. A refund, reshipment, carrier contact or address amendment must not be treated as completed solely because it was requested or accepted for review.
