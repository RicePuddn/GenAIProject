## 5. Order status and tracking

### ODR-OrderStatusTracking-5.1 Order and parcel status

An order may be processing, shipped, partially fulfilled, delivered or cancelled. These statuses describe different stages and must be considered together with the associated shipment records. In particular: (a) processing does not necessarily mean warehouse work has not begun; (b) a shipping label does not establish carrier pickup; (c) delivery of one parcel does not establish delivery of all items; and (d) several tracking numbers do not necessarily indicate duplicate orders. Order references, shipment references and carrier tracking numbers identify different records. Customer-specific order, address and payment information is available only after the customer's authority to access the order has been established; possession of an order reference alone is insufficient.

### ODR-OrderStatusTracking-5.2 Missing or invalid tracking information

A missing scan or invalid tracking number may reflect an unupdated record, an incorrect reference or a carrier-system issue. It does not, by itself, establish cancellation or loss. The latest confirmed shipping event and any discrepancy should be used when enquiring about progress. An update, carrier contact or expedited handling must not be presented as completed unless it has been confirmed.

### ODR-OrderStatusTracking-5.3 Estimated delivery date

The latest estimated date recorded for the affected shipment is the applicable estimate. If no shipment-specific date exists, a general period under section 2.1 may be given only where the service and pickup date are established. An estimated date is not proof that delivery occurred. Missing dates and conflicting status information require clarification or review, not substitution of an assumed arrival date.
