## 3. Shipping fees

### SHP-ShippingFees-3.1 Applicable charge

The shipping fee for a prospective purchase is the charge confirmed at checkout. For an existing purchase, the applicable fee is the amount recorded on that order, including any recorded discount and tax components. Historical charges are not recalculated using current catalog prices. A free-shipping threshold or loyalty benefit applies only where it formed part of the purchase terms. A customer disputing a shipping charge may submit the order reference and relevant payment information for review.

### SHP-ShippingFees-3.2 Separate parcels and additional charges

An order may be divided into several parcels without creating an additional shipping charge. The original fee must not be multiplied by the number of shipments or tracking numbers. A suspected duplicate charge is subject to payment review. Two pending authorizations do not necessarily represent two settled charges, and the same amount will not be refunded twice.

### SHP-ShippingFees-3.3 Shipping fees on cancellation or return

For an accepted full cancellation before fulfillment, the refund calculation includes the original recorded shipping and tax components, subject to the captured balance and earlier refunds. For an accepted whole-order return due to store fault, the recorded outbound shipping fee is refundable once. For change-of-mind returns, the original outbound fee is normally retained. A partial return does not automatically make the whole outbound fee refundable. Delay alone does not establish an automatic entitlement to compensation or a shipping-fee refund. Disputed fees and missing allocations require review.
