## ODR-Application-1 Application

Orders for physical goods are accepted for delivery to serviceable Singapore addresses, subject to the delivery option, charges and conditions confirmed at checkout. This Policy governs delivery services, shipping fees, changes to orders, tracking, partial deliveries and delivery problems. A request to change or cancel an order does not constitute confirmation that the change or cancellation has taken effect. All dates use Singapore time. Calendar days include weekends and public holidays. Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and Singapore public holidays. Delivery periods and service targets are estimates unless an express commitment has been confirmed for the order.

## 2. Shipping options

### SHP-ShippingOptions-2.1 Available delivery services

The following doorstep delivery services may be available at checkout: (i) standard delivery, normally taking two (2) to five (5) business days after carrier pickup; and (ii) express delivery, normally taking one (1) to two (2) business days after carrier pickup. Availability depends on the delivery address and the goods purchased. The service applicable to an existing order is the service recorded for that order; the courier's name alone does not establish the service purchased. International delivery, lockers, collection points, installation and scheduled delivery appointments are not included in the standard offering. A request concerning a service not confirmed for the order requires review rather than an assumed entitlement.

### SHP-ShippingOptions-2.2 Delivery periods and appointments

The estimated periods in section 2.1 begin when the carrier accepts the parcel, not when the order is placed, paid or assigned a label. Where the shipment has an updated estimated delivery date, that shipment-specific date takes precedence over a general service estimate. A specific arrival time cannot be reserved through the standard delivery service. The customer should arrange for an authorized recipient where necessary. An estimate does not guarantee delivery on a particular date, and an unsupported revised date must not be presented as confirmed.

Related clauses: SHP-ShippingOptions-2.1.

### SHP-ShippingOptions-2.3 Changes to the shipping option

A customer may request a service change before fulfillment begins under section 4.1. A change is subject to: (a) availability for the replacement service; (b) serviceability of the delivery address; (c) acceptance of any revised shipping charge; and (d) confirmation that the amendment has been recorded. Once fulfillment begins, a service upgrade or expedited handling is not guaranteed and requires human review. A request alone does not amend the purchased service.

Related clauses: ODR-OrderChangeCancel-4.1.
